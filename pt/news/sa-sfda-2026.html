Médio Oriente 2026-07-20

Saudi SFDA updates medical device requirements and issues risk communication guidance

SFDA revised or issued several medical device requirements and guidance documents in July and August 2026.

Pontos principais

  • MDS-REQ 8 v4 (advertising approval) carries a version date of 20 July 2026.
  • MDS-REQ 3 v3 (safe use of devices in healthcare facilities) is dated 30 July 2026.
  • MDS-G29 v1, guidance on risk communication, is dated 9 July 2026.
  • MDS-G28 (bundling criteria in one MDMA application) is dated 3 May 2026.

Resumo de comunicados oficiais, apenas para referência. Os documentos originais prevalecem.
