Risk Management

We run the whole ISO 14971 process across the product life cycle, from the risk management plan to residual risk evaluation and post-market feedback, and make sure the results connect to design requirements and verification records.

What we do

  • Risk management planWe set risk acceptability criteria, responsibilities and review points before analysis starts.
  • Risk analysisWe identify hazards, hazardous situations and harms and analyze them with suitable methods such as FMEA and FTA.
  • Risk controlWe derive controls in the order of design, protective measures and safety information, and verify their effectiveness.
  • Residual risk evaluationWe evaluate individual and overall residual risk and the benefit-risk balance.
  • Risk management file and reportWe prepare a traceable risk management file and report.
  • Post-market linkWe set up how production and post-production information feeds back into risk management.

When to contact us

  • You need to create a risk management file for the first time
  • You must update risk management after a design change or software update
  • You were told that risk management results do not link to design and test records

Related standards and regulations

  • ISO 14971:2019
  • ISO/TR 24971:2020
  • IEC 62304
