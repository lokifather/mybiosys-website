Usability Engineering

Following IEC 62366-1, we define users, use environments and critical tasks, design formative and summative evaluations, and build the file that shows risks arising from use error are acceptable.

What we do

  • Use specificationWe document intended users, use environments and the operating principle.
  • Use-related risk analysisWe identify hazardous situations and critical tasks and link them to the risk management file.
  • Formative evaluationWe plan evaluations that improve the user interface during development and support their execution.
  • Summative evaluationWe write the protocol and report for a summative evaluation with representative users and environments.
  • Usability engineering fileWe organize the whole process into a traceable file.

When to contact us

  • You need usability evidence for a product with software or controls
  • You need usability documents for an FDA submission or EU MDR certification
  • Use errors keep appearing in market complaints

Related standards and regulations

  • IEC 62366-1:2015+A1:2020
  • FDA human factors guidance
  • MFDS usability guideline
