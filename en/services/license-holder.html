Korea License Holding

To sell a medical device in Korea, an overseas manufacturer must obtain approval, certification or notification through a Korean importer. We act as your Korean license holder from approval through post-market management, so you can focus on the Korean market.

What we do

  • Approval strategyWe confirm the device class, the approval, certification or notification route and the documents needed, then set the schedule.
  • ApplicationsWe prepare and submit applications and respond to questions from MFDS and the review bodies.
  • Korean-language documentsWe prepare the technical file, labeling and instructions for use in Korean to meet local requirements.
  • Import quality managementWe support compliance with importer quality management and overseas manufacturing site review requirements.
  • Post-market managementWe handle adverse event reporting, recalls, change approvals and renewals, and UDI management.
  • Communication with youWe report progress and needs to you in English.

When to contact us

  • You are an overseas manufacturer entering the Korean market for the first time
  • You need to change your Korean importer or transfer the license holder
  • You want to lighten post-market and change notification work after approval

Related standards and regulations

  • Medical Devices Act (Korea)
  • KGMP (Korean GMP for medical devices)
  • MFDS technical file review
  • UDI
