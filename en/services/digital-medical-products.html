Digital Medical Products Act

Under the Digital Medical Products Act, in force in Korea since 24 January 2025, we determine whether your product is a digital medical device or a digital medical and health support device, then organize approval, certification of the good management system and cybersecurity as one path.

What we do

  • Scope and classificationWe check whether the software function is a digital medical device or a health support device, and how it is classified and graded.
  • Approval and review documentsWe organize the submission documents that approval review of software and AI-based products needs.
  • Good management system certificationWe build the system to the MFDS guideline on good management system certification and prepare for it.
  • Life cycle, risk and usability linkWe make the results of IEC 62304, ISO 14971 and IEC 62366-1 agree with the approval documents.
  • Cybersecurity documentsWe write documents that reflect the MFDS cybersecurity review guideline.
  • Review responsesWe support the answers to questions and requests for additional information during review.

When to contact us

  • You need to know whether your software or AI product falls under the Digital Medical Products Act
  • You are preparing digital medical device approval or good management system certification
  • You want to check an existing software device against the new framework
