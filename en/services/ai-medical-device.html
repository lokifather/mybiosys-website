AI Medical Device Support

For AI-enabled medical devices and software, the approval file has to cover the algorithm, training data, performance validation and model change control. From Korean approval to checking US and EU requirements, we organize this so it agrees with your risk management and quality system records.

What we do

  • Scope and classificationWe check whether the AI function is a medical device or a digital medical device, and what its class and approval route are.
  • Approval and review documentsWe organize the algorithm description and performance evaluation to fit the MFDS guideline on approval review of AI medical devices.
  • Data management recordsWe document the source and composition of training, validation and test data, how they are separated, and the bias review.
  • Performance validation and clinical evidenceWe set evaluation metrics and a test plan, and support the design of the clinical performance evaluation.
  • Model change controlWe plan how retraining and updates are managed and how change approvals are handled, including the US PCCP approach.
  • Link to risk, quality and overseas rulesWe link ISO 14971, the machine learning risk specification ISO/TS 24971-2 and IEC 62304 to the ISO 13485 quality system. We refer to IEC PAS 63621 for data management and EN 18286 for the AI quality system under the EU AI Act, and check how the EU requirements relate.

When to contact us

  • You are preparing the first approval file for a product with an AI function
  • You need to decide which change procedure applies when you retrain or update a model
  • You want Korean, US and EU requirements compared in one pass
