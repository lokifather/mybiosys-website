Quality Management System

We build your quality management system on ISO 13485 and fold in market-specific requirements such as Korean KGMP, EU MDR, MDSAP and the US QMSR, then stay with you until you are ready for certification and audits.

What we do

  • Gap assessmentWe compare your current procedures and records against the requirements and identify gaps.
  • Documentation designWe structure the quality manual, procedures and records for your size and product.
  • Core process implementationWe implement design and development, purchasing, nonconformity, CAPA and complaint handling.
  • Operational supportWe help you run internal audits and management reviews in practice.
  • Audit supportWe support certification audits, KGMP audits and the response to audit findings.

When to contact us

  • You need to establish a QMS before manufacturing or import approval
  • You are preparing for audits in several markets at once
  • You want to resolve recurring audit findings at the root

Related standards and regulations

  • ISO 13485:2016
  • KGMP (Korean GMP for medical devices)
  • MDSAP
  • 21 CFR 820(QMSR)
  • EU MDR Annex IX
