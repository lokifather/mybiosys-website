How we work

The same sequence, whatever the project size.

  1. Assess

    We review the product, target markets and existing documents, then list the requirements and gaps.

  2. Design

    We define the structure of documents and processes, the schedule and the roles, and design the traceability.

  3. Write and build

    We write the documents and build the processes, checking consistency across documents as we go.

  4. Support reviews

    We handle questions and deficiency requests from notified bodies and regulators, and audit findings.
