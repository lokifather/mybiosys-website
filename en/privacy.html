Privacy Policy

mybiosys ("the Company") processes only the minimum personal information needed to respond to inquiries.

1. Information collected

Name, company, email address and the content of your message.

2. Purpose

To confirm and reply to your inquiry and to keep a record of the consultation.

3. Retention

We keep it for one year after the inquiry is closed and then delete it, unless the law requires a different period.

4. Disclosure to third parties and processors

We do not provide personal information to third parties. However, the contact form is delivered through an external service, Web3Forms, and what you enter passes through it to our email. That provider processes the transmitted data on our behalf, which may happen on servers outside Korea. If you prefer not to use it, please email us directly instead of using the form.

5. Your rights

You may ask at any time to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal information. Contact us at the address below.

6. Right to decline

You may decline to consent. In that case you can email us directly instead of using the form.

7. Contact

Email: contact@mybiosys.com
