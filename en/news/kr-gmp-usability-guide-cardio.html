Korea 2026-11-18

MFDS posts usability guidance under medical device GMP standards (심혈관용 기계기구), guidance for applicants

On 18 November 2026 the Ministry of Food and Drug Safety registered a guidance for applicants on usability under the medical device GMP standards (심혈관용 기계기구), intended to let manufacturers apply usability requirements easily.

Key points

  • The document is titled 「의료기기 GMP 기준 중 사용적합성 가이드라인(심혈관용 기계기구)」 (usability guideline under the medical device GMP standards, 심혈관용 기계기구; guidance for applicants) and carries registration number 안내서-1547-01.
  • Both the notification date and the registration date are 18 November 2026.
  • The post states that the guidance was prepared so that manufacturers can easily apply the usability requirements that ensure the safe and correct use of medical devices.
  • The document states that it presents quality management examples in which medical device manufacturing sites of the GMP product group "심혈관용 기계 기구" applied the usability requirements; it contains two cases, a powered cardiopulmonary resuscitator and a pulse oximeter. It states that it has no external legal effect.
