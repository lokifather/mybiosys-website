FDA issues final guidance on PMA and HDE modular review

In January 2025 FDA posted the final guidance "Premarket Approval Application and Humanitarian Device Exemption Modular Review".

Key points

  • The document is final guidance for industry and FDA staff under docket number FDA-2024-D-5332. The issuing office is CDRH and CBER and the page shows a posting date of 13 January 2025.
  • It provides information on the PMA and HDE modular review program and on the procedures for submitting or reviewing a modular PMA or HDE.
  • The page says comments can be submitted at any time at regulations.gov under that docket.
