FDA issues final guidance on eCopy program for medical device submissions

In December 2025 FDA posted the final guidance "eCopy Program for Medical Device Submissions".

Key points

  • The document is final guidance for industry and FDA staff under docket number FDA-2018-N-0628. The issuing office is CDRH and CBER and the page shows a posting date of 3 December 2025.
  • It describes how FDA implements the eCopy Program under section 745A(b) of the FD&C Act. The page lists Pre-Submissions and submissions under FD&C Act sections 510(k), 513(f)(2)(A), 515(c), 515(d), 515(f), 520(g), 520(m) or 564 and PHS Act section 351, and states that eSTAR is the current program for solely electronic submissions and that eCopies are required for a submission type until eSTAR is implemented for it.
  • The page says this is an updated version of the guidance issued on 27 April 2020, with updates clarifying the intersection with the eSTAR Program and the availability of the CDRH Portal, and minor changes reflecting modern technical standards such as a larger acceptable file size.
  • The page says comments can be submitted at any time at regulations.gov under that docket.
