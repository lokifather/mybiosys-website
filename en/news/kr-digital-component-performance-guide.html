MFDS establishes guidance on performance evaluation of digital medical device components (guidance for applicants)

On 16 September 2026 the Ministry of Food and Drug Safety established guidance for applicants explaining which digital medical device components are subject to performance evaluation and how to prepare the attached documents.

Key points

  • The document is titled "Guideline on Performance Evaluation of Digital Medical Device Components" (guidance for applicants) and carries registration number 안내서-1527-01. Its notification date is 16 September 2026 and registration date 14 September 2026; it was prepared by the Medical Device Evaluation Department and states that it has no external legal effect.
  • The component performance evaluation system was introduced with the enforcement of the Digital Medical Products Act in January 2025; it is described as evaluating components that can affect performance, such as AI algorithms and sensors, as independent units, and simplifying the approval (certification) procedure for digital medical products that use evaluated components.
  • Subject to evaluation are AI-based programs implementing medical functions, sensors measuring biosignals and similar (including related software), and items the Minister recognizes as equivalent. Excluded are components usable as a finished product approved or certified as one, those that do not perform independently, and those whose safety can be evaluated only as a finished product.
  • The review procedure is: submitting the application (Form No. 45 of the Enforcement Rule), MFDS review, reply on the review result (supplementation may be requested within a period of up to 30 days; a result report is issued if suitable), and, where applicable, additional performance evaluation by a specialized body, whose cost the applicant pays to that body.
