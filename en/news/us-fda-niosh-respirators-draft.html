FDA issues draft guidance on compliance policy for premarket and other requirements for certain NIOSH-approved air-purifying respirators

In April 2026 FDA posted the draft guidance "Compliance Policy Regarding Premarket and Other Requirements for Certain NIOSH Approved Air-Purifying Respirators".

Key points

  • The document is draft guidance for industry and FDA staff under docket number FDA-2025-D-7121. The issuing office is CDRH and the page shows a posting date of 17 April 2026. The page states it is not for implementation and contains non-binding recommendations.
  • FDA proposes a compliance policy and gives information on NIOSH-approved respirators under 42 CFR part 84.
  • It covers surgical N95 respirators and N95 filtering facepiece respirators (FFRs) classified under 21 CFR 878.4040; other non-surgical NIOSH-approved respirators, including powered air-purifying respirators (PAPRs), non-powered particulate FFRs and reusable elastomeric half and full facepiece respirators; and FFRs for use by the general public in public health medical emergencies, classified under 21 CFR 880.6260.
  • The page says comments can be submitted at any time at regulations.gov under that docket.
