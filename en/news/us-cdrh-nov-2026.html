FDA CDRH: new guidance and notices in November; comments open on FY2027 guidance list

CDRH posted new guidance and Federal Register notices in November 2026 and is taking comments on its FY2027 guidance list until 30 November.

Key points

  • 9 Nov: guidance on validating certain IVDs for emerging pathogens during a Section 564 declared emergency.
  • 4 Nov: Federal Register notices on classifying mild cognitive impairment prognostic assessment software and a wearable vibration device for orthopedic use.
  • 10 Nov: notice reclassifying immunohistochemistry test systems used with a corresponding approved oncology therapeutic.
  • CDRH accepts comments on its FY2027 A- and B-list guidance until 30 November 2026. In FY2026 it published 7 of the 11 guidances on the list.
