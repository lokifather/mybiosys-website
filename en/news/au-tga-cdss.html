Australia TGA updates CDSS software guidance; software and AI reforms continue

TGA's guidance on regulating clinical decision support software is dated 10 November 2026, and its software and AI reforms page was updated on 30 September.

Key points

  • The guidance explains when CDSS software is exempt from inclusion in the ARTG, dated 10 November.
  • The AI review engaged over 600 stakeholders, produced 14 findings and confirmed current regulation remains broadly appropriate.
  • Follow-up work includes disclosure requirements for AI that is a medical device, software-specific mandatory statements and showing the ARTG number in advertising.
  • From 1 July 2026 medical devices require a UDI, starting with class IIb and III.

Worth checking

The reforms are in progress. Check TGA's pages for final requirements.
