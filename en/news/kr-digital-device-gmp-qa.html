NIDS posts Q&A guide on manufacturing and quality management of digital medical devices (guidance for applicants)

On 5 November 2026 the National Institute of Medical Device Safety Information posted, on its guideline board, a Q&A guide on manufacturing and quality management of digital medical devices issued under the supervision of the Ministry of Food and Drug Safety.

Key points

  • The document is titled "Q&A on Manufacturing and Quality Management Standards for Digital Medical Devices" (guidance for applicants, 「디지털의료기기 제조 및 품질관리 질의응답집」) and was issued under the supervision of the Ministry of Food and Drug Safety. On the MFDS post, the registration number is 안내서-1526-01 and the notification and registration dates are 10 September 2026.
  • It is addressed to manufacturers and importers of digital medical devices; its stated purpose is to help them understand manufacturing and quality management standards and prepare for GMP review.
  • It has five parts: applying for digital GMP review; types of review (initial, change, periodic, alternative); review criteria and methods; quality management system review criteria; and digital medical device software review criteria.
  • The document records its establishment date as 10 September 2026 and states that it has no external legal effect.
