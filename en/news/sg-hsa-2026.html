Southeast Asia 2026-10-01

Singapore HSA: GN-15-R15 registration guidance (Oct 2026) and reliance programme with Malaysia

HSA posted the 15th revision of its device registration guidance GN-15 dated October 2026, and the HSA-MDA reliance programme went fully live on 1 March 2026.

Key points

  • GN-15-R15 and its annexes (letter of authorisation, marketing history, safety declaration) are dated October 2026.
  • Devices registered with HSA may get faster review in Malaysia, and MDA-registered devices a shortened HSA pathway.
  • Applications for the Registration Support Initiative for unregistered devices are open until 31 December 2026; eligible devices get evaluation fee waivers.

A summary of official announcements for reference. The original documents are authoritative.
