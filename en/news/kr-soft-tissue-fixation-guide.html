Korea 2026-11-06

MFDS establishes licensing and review guideline for soft tissue fixation devices (guidance for applicants)

On 6 November 2026 the Ministry of Food and Drug Safety posted that it has established the licensing and review guideline for soft tissue fixation devices (guidance for applicants).

Key points

  • The document is titled "Licensing and Review Guideline for Soft Tissue Fixation Devices" (guidance for applicants, 「연조직고정용장치 허가·심사 가이드라인」) and carries registration number 안내서-1544-01. Its revision history records establishment on 6 November 2026.
  • It has four parts: general matters; how to prepare the licensing and review request; requirements for attached licensing and review materials; and references.
  • The document states that soft tissue fixation devices and special-material soft tissue fixation devices are Class 3 and Class 4 medical devices respectively, and that technical documentation must be submitted when applying for licensing review.
  • The document states that it has no external legal effect and names the Orthopedic and Rehabilitation Devices Division, Medical Device Evaluation Department, National Institute of Food and Drug Safety Evaluation as the contact.
