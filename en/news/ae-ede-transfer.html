UAE: MOHAP transfers selected services to the Emirates Drug Establishment (EDE)

On 5 January 2026 MOHAP announced the transfer of selected services to the Emirates Drug Establishment.

Key points

  • EDE regulates medical products including medical devices, free zones included.
  • Transferred services include marketing authorizations and approvals or permits to import, export or re-export medical products.
  • MOHAP pages tell applicants to submit requests for those services through the EDE website.
