Thailand FDA digitalizes device application forms and record-keeping and reporting criteria

In 2026 Thai FDA issued three notifications on device manufacture, import and sale, digitalizing forms and reporting.

Kernpunkte

  • 5 March: revised establishment registration forms, with scope adjusted to cover standalone software.
  • 24 April: improved criteria for record-keeping and reporting on manufacture, import and sale, introducing electronic systems.
  • The aim is to cut unnecessary reporting while keeping information needed for consumer protection.

Zusammenfassung offizieller Veröffentlichungen, nur zur Orientierung. Maßgeblich sind die Originaldokumente.
