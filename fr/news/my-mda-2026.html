Asie du Sud-Est 2026-05-01

Malaysia MDA: change management and labelling (e-IFU) guidance; procurement circular 1/2026

MDA issued a first-edition change management guidance and a 7th-edition labelling guidance in May 2026; procurement circular 1/2026 took effect on 28 January.

Points clés

  • The change management guidance (MDA/GD/0072), first edition, is dated May 2026.
  • The 7th-edition labelling guidance (MDA/GD/0026) adds e-labelling definitions and requires a documented risk analysis before implementing electronic IFUs.
  • The definition of medical device guidance, 2nd edition (MDA/GD/0006), is dated 30 March 2026.
  • Circular 1/2026 on procurement of devices for healthcare facilities took effect on 28 January 2026.

Résumé d'annonces officielles, à titre de référence uniquement. Les documents d'origine font foi.
